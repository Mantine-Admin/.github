# Getting help

- **Questions, ideas and feature requests**: [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/Mantine-Admin/discussions).
- **Bugs**: open an issue in the relevant repository, with a minimal reproduction.
- **Security vulnerabilities**: follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md). Never use public channels.

Mantine Admin is maintained on a best-effort basis. We don't provide private or paid support.
